Status: proposed for approval before workflow creation

## Source Context

- RCSZilla positions itself around SMS and email campaigns, Android device SMS gateway, two-way inbox, appointment reminders, delivery reports, and WhatsApp AI auto-replies.
- The n8n community node supports queueing messages, queue status checks, pending queue reads, delivery-state updates, inbound reply submission, outgoing logs, and AI auto-reply automation.
- Workflow ideas should avoid unsolicited messaging. Templates should be framed around consent-based, transactional, reminder, support, and opted-in marketing use cases.
